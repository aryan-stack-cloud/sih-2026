PUSHPAK — Smart Scan: The Animated Solution

This 119-second narrated 2D film shows why a receiver must scan a wide spectrum through a narrow instantaneous window, then explains six scheduling approaches with moving visual examples. It follows the scan decision from evidence to retuning and shows two simulation input sources.

The measured comparison is the documented held-out Scenario B result: Index detection probability 0.1334 versus fixed sweep 0.1092, a 22% relative increase, with both at 0.9667 emitter reach. Bandit's detection/coverage tradeoff is from a separate evaluation. Training wall time was not recorded comparably. The film presents simulation results, not a field RF demonstration.
