# v4 — PUSHPAK as an Infographics-Show explainer: "The Guard Who Always Missed the Thief"

## Style research (grounding)

- YouTube blocked automated viewing (empty page body for bots), so no video was watched frame-by-frame. Style is grounded in published descriptions: The Infographics Show (15M+ subs) = narrator-driven motion-infographic videos that simplify complex topics into story-like, entertaining explainers with a lively conversational narrator (animotionsstudio.com breakdown; channel statement: "animated motion infographic videos, made in a fun and entertaining way").
- Format DNA applied here: hook question → relatable everyday scenario with simple flat cartoon characters → "let's swap X for Y" mapping to the real technical system → numbered solution steps → measured payoff → real product demo. Narrator carries the story; on-screen text is keywords, labels, and numbers only.
- All characters, rooms, and props are original flat SVG/CSS shapes (no copyrighted assets, no footage).

## Analogy contract (kept honest on screen)

- Museum guard = receiver (watches 2 rooms = listens to 2 bands). Thief = emitter (strikes one room briefly = illuminates one band briefly). Patrol route = sweep schedule. Notebook/ranking/dice = beliefs/index/dither.
- Scene 4 explicitly labels the mapping so nobody mistakes the cartoon for the claim. Verified numbers stay attached to their real experiments: fixed sweep 0.000 (sync-trap run), bandit 0.710 vs index 0.967 (Scenario B hold-out seeds).

## Voiceover (Kokoro am_adam, conversational explainer tone)

- S1: "Six rooms, one guard, and a very sneaky thief. Every night, the guard misses him. Why?"
- S2: "The guard can watch only two rooms at once. A catch needs the right room, at the right time. Miss one, and the thief walks free."
- S3: "The guard patrols the same route every night: rooms one, two, three, four. The thief notices, and always strikes the room the guard just left. Night after night, caught: zero."
- S4: "Swap the museum for radio spectrum: rooms become frequency bands, the guard is the receiver, the thief is the emitter. Same trap, same blindness: detection, zero."
- S5: "So we gave the guard a brain. Step one: a notebook. Which rooms get hit? Step two: a ranking. Check risky rooms first. Step three: dice. Mix up the patrol so it can't be predicted. Catches jump from zero point seven one zero, to zero point nine six seven."
- S6: "This is the real product. Press run the demo: the dashboard streams a live simulation, the waterfall, the policies racing, and Index pulling ahead on the same seed."
- S7: "Pushpak. The guard that learned where to look. Simulation only."
