2 · Attention vs activity

Is listening time going where the signals are? Up = share of all transmissions on that band. Down = share of the receiver's looks. Match = overlap of the two shapes (100% = listening exactly where the signals are).

3 · Why this band?

Every Index decision decomposes into named terms. This is the explainability the spec promised.

Decision source per step: SEARCH = random draw among uncharacterised bands · TRACK = highest index · COVERAGE = revisit deadline forced the band.

4 · What the scheduler believes (from the periodicity estimator)

Dial = where each band's emitter is in its cycle. Filled arc = confidence; dot = phase; dashed = no claim yet; outlined = being listened to now.

5 · Learning curve: when does the scheduler start winning?

Index is a sweep for its first ~200 steps, then improves for the rest of the run. A 160-step demo can never show it; a 600–2000 step run does. Windowed detection rate, mean of 4 worlds.
